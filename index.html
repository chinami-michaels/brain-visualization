Measured anatomy, rendered as mesh

The tubes follow sampled streamlines from the HCP1065 population atlas by Yeh (2022), derived from diffusion MRI of 1,065 Human Connectome Project participants. Streamlines were converted to tube geometry at build time; their routes were not hand-drawn.
